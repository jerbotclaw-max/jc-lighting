The Lineup

Twelve fixtures, one open platform

Every JC edition ships with the same electronics contract: GetBrighter-class emitters (CRI ≥97, R9 > 90, TM-30 RF ≥90), tunable white 2700–6500K (RGBW where noted), flicker-free >25 kHz PWM dimming — <1% at every level, 0–100% range — from a 100–240V driver, Matter over WiFi (or Thread where noted), BLE provisioning, USB-C service port, and a replaceable LED board. Firmware is one build — JC Light OS — with a per-fixture profile.

JC Bend after "Bend" · Visual Comfort Signature · sconce family 8/12/20", Square, Tall

$499–$899→kit from $125

A continuous tube of light bent into a ring, square, or tall loop. The original is a bent steel tube with plated finishes; the JC edition bends structural 6063 aluminum in one piece and caps it in machined solid brass.

JC redesign
